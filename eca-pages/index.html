Our history

Representing contractors since 1991.

ECA represents, protects, and advances the interests of construction companies and allied tradespeople while promoting professional and ethical industry development.

About ECA
  1. 1991

    ECA's roots were established through a coalition of local contractors.

  2. 2013

    ECA played a leading role in the development of the Construction Industry Council Act.

  3. 2018

    Eswatini adopted its national name, marking a new chapter for local institutions.

  4. 2019

    The Association adopted its current ECA identity.

  5. 2020

    The Women in Construction initiative strengthened focus on participation in the sector.

  6. 2021

    ECA continued its construction industry advocacy.

  7. Present

    ECA continues representing contractors and advancing Eswatini's construction industry.
